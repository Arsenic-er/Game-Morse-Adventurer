# Chapter One: approved narrative to real play

The player approved the review slice's visual direction and Chinese narrative. This implementation preserves that material and replaces the review-only shortcut with the existing production QSO workflow. The standalone review URL remains unchanged.

## Scope

- Discoverable Chapter One entry on Home and on the accepted Mission Center card.
- Explicit mission acceptance before the opening; a small versioned presentation bookmark scoped to that acceptance.
- Opening artwork and narrative, followed by the actual station and physical Space/Z/X input.
- No guaranteed respondent, fabricated RST, scripted successful QSO, or separate reward path.
- The closing narrative reads a post-acceptance ordinary QSO that is present in the saved log, settlement ledger, and matching mission event.
- Mission reward uses the existing `claimMission` transaction; presentation never settles money.
- Reload resumes the opening or the real saved ending. Old saves gain no progress or rewards.
- Portraits remain off-air. Production radio screens continue to use environmental art only.

## Verification contract

Test old-save migration, acceptance-scoped bookmarks, invalid/skipped beats, missing/old/event/unsettled logs, duplicate presentation updates, and duplicate claims. Render opening and ending with the real player and counterpart facts. Browser QA must key a real ordinary QSO, save it, return to the story, claim once, and reload without duplicate rewards. Also check settings pause, failure/retry, and the unchanged review route.

This task does not expand Chapters Two–Fifteen, unlock future technology nodes, alter the NPC pool, merge main, or publish a release.
